# AporiaX Community Code of Conduct

AporiaX is built around uncertainty, evidence, and respectful review. Everyone
participating in this repository, its Issues, Discussions, and related community
spaces is expected to help keep those spaces constructive and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient with people of different backgrounds and skill levels.
- Critique ideas and implementations rather than people.
- Share enough evidence to make technical claims reviewable.
- Respect privacy. Never publish another person's credentials, private files, logs,
  or identifying information.
- Accept correction, disclose mistakes, and help repair harm when possible.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption,
doxxing, credential exposure, and sustained bad-faith behavior are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct or security concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact
method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock
threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and promptly as practical.

This code applies in official project spaces and when someone is publicly representing
AporiaX. It is informed by the Contributor Covenant and common open-source community
standards.
